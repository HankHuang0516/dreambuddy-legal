Dream Buddy（夢話夥伴）隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 7 月 11 日

Dream Buddy（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）是一款陪伴使用者記錄與探索夢境的 AI 對話應用程式。我們重視您的隱私，本政策說明我們如何處理您的資料。

1. 我們收集的資料

  • 對話內容：您在本應用程式中輸入的文字訊息會傳送至我們的 AI 推論伺服器以產生回應。這些訊息以匿名裝置識別碼（device ID）處理，我們不會將其與您的姓名、Email 或其他個人識別資料連結。
  • 使用量統計：我們記錄您每日的訊息數量以執行免費額度限制（每日 N 則）。此資料以裝置為單位彙總，不含訊息內容。
  • 裝置技術資訊：作業系統版本、應用程式版本、Android 廣告識別碼（僅在您觀看獎勵廣告時使用，見第 3 節）。

本應用程式不收集：您的真實姓名、Email、電話、精確位置、聯絡人、相簿、麥克風、相機。

2. 我們如何使用這些資料

  • 提供 AI 對話功能
  • 執行每日免費額度
  • 診斷應用程式錯誤與效能問題
  • 符合廣告平台的合規要求（若您觀看獎勵廣告）

我們不會將您的對話內容用於訓練 AI 模型、賣給第三方，或用於廣告個人化。

3. 廣告與第三方服務

本應用程式使用 Google AdMob 提供獎勵式廣告以延長您的每日免費額度。當您觀看廣告時，Google 可能會依 Google 隱私權政策 收集裝置資訊、廣告識別碼與互動資料。您可以在裝置設定中重設或停用 Android 廣告識別碼。

目前使用之第三方服務：

  • Google AdMob（獎勵式廣告）
  • EClaw AI Platform（AI 對話推論後端；不儲存訊息內容超過完成回應所需時間）

4. 資料儲存與安全

對話內容以裝置為單位、匿名化方式暫存於伺服器記憶體，僅用於產生本次回應，回應完成後即從記憶體釋放，不會寫入永久儲存空間。每日使用量統計以匿名裝置識別碼保存最多 24 小時後歸零。

我們採用產業標準的傳輸加密（HTTPS/TLS）保護資料在您裝置與伺服器之間的往返。

5. 兒童隱私

本應用程式不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計。我們不會蓄意收集 13 歲以下兒童的資料。若您是家長且發現我們可能收集了您孩子的資料，請透過下方 Email 聯絡我們，我們會立即刪除。

6. 您的權利

您可以隨時刪除本應用程式，此舉將移除裝置上所有本地資料。若您希望我們刪除伺服器端與您的裝置識別碼相關的任何資料，請 Email 我們。

7. 政策變更

若本政策有重大變更，我們會於本頁面公告並更新頂端的「最後更新」日期。持續使用本應用程式即代表您接受更新後的政策。

Privacy Policy — Dream Buddy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Dream Buddy is an AI companion app that helps users record and explore their dreams. We value your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Chat messages: Text you type in the app is sent to our AI inference server to generate a response. Messages are processed under an anonymous device ID and are never linked to your name, email, or other personally identifiable information.
  • Usage counts: We log your daily message count to enforce the free quota (N messages per day). This data is aggregated per device and does not include message content.
  • Device technical info: OS version, app version, and the Android Advertising ID (only when you watch a rewarded ad; see Section 3).

We do NOT collect: your real name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To power the AI conversation feature
  • To enforce the daily free quota
  • To diagnose app errors and performance issues
  • To comply with ad platform requirements (only if you watch rewarded ads)

We do NOT use your conversation content to train AI models, sell it to third parties, or use it for ad personalization.

3. Ads and Third Parties

The app uses Google AdMob to serve rewarded ads that extend your daily free quota. When you watch an ad, Google may collect device info, the Advertising ID, and interaction data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of the Android Advertising ID in your device settings.

Current third parties:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded ads)
  • EClaw AI Platform (AI conversation inference; does not retain message content beyond the time needed to generate a reply)

4. Storage and Security

Chat content is held anonymously in server memory only for the duration of generating the current reply and is then released; it is not written to persistent storage. Per-device daily usage counts are retained for at most 24 hours before reset. We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS) between your device and our servers.

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe we may have collected data from your child, please email us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

You can delete the app at any time; this removes all local data on your device. If you want us to delete anything associated with your device ID on the server side, email us.

7. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced on this page and the "Last updated" date at the top will be updated. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
